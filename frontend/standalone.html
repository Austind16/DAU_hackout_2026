1. Problem Statement

Households with rooftop solar overproduce during peak sunlight hours and export surplus power to the grid at low feed-in tariffs. Nearby neighbors during outages or high-demand hours pay high retail rates or suffer blackouts. SolarShare enables neighbor-to-neighbor direct trading over local microgrids.

2. Core Goal

  • Prosumers sell surplus energy at fair prices above grid tariff.
  • Consumers buy cheap power directly from neighbors during grid outages.
  • Dynamic pricing based on real supply, demand, and grid proximity.
  • Housing society achieves high energy resilience and self-sufficiency.

3. User Roles

Prosumer: Solar owner selling surplus
Consumer: Buyer in grid outage
RWA Admin: Society generation view
Utility: Reduced peak grid load

4. Hackathon Tech Stack

Tailwind CSS • SVG 3D Isometric Engine • Chart.js • DLT Audit Trail • Reverse Pulse Flow Mesh Protocol
